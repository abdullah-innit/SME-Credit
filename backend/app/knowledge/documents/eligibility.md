## Chunk KB-ELIG-001: Core SME Eligibility Criteria
- **Chunk ID:** KB-ELIG-001
- **Category:** eligibility
- **Title:** Core SME Eligibility Criteria
- **Tags:** eligibility, business age, turnover, residency, legal structure, اہلیت, شرائط اہلیت

### Overview
To qualify for working capital financing through SME Credit, an enterprise must satisfy the following fundamental operational benchmarks:
1. **Operating Vintage:** Minimum 12 consecutive months of verifiable commercial operations (24 months preferred for Tier A pricing).
2. **Turnover Threshold:** Minimum annual revenue of PKR 1,200,000 (PKR 100,000 average monthly cash turnover) recorded through active financial accounts.
3. **Legal Entity:** Registered as an individual sole proprietorship, registered partnership, or private limited company in Pakistan.
4. **Residency & Citizenship:** The primary business owner/signatory must hold a valid CNIC / SNIC and reside in Pakistan.
5. **Active Bank or Wallet Footprint:** Must possess at least one commercial bank account or merchant digital wallet with active transaction history.

## Chunk KB-ELIG-002: Sector & Geographic Coverage
- **Chunk ID:** KB-ELIG-002

- **Title:** Sector and Geographic Eligibility
- **Tags:** sector, retail, garments, clinics, agriculture, logistics, geography, provinces

### Sector Coverage
Financing is available across diverse real-economy sectors:
- Retail & Wholesale distribution
- Garments, Textiles & Apparel
- Food & Beverage / Cafes / Bakeries
- Agri-processing, inputs & Livestock trade
- IT, Software houses & Freelance service bureaus
- Logistics, Freight & Fleet transport operators
- Light Manufacturing & Fabrication workshops
- Healthcare clinics & Pharmacies

### Geographic Coverage
Businesses located in all provinces and territories of Pakistan (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan, and Azad Jammu & Kashmir) are eligible.

## Chunk KB-ELIG-003: Thin-File & Women-Led SME Affirmative Criteria
- **Chunk ID:** KB-ELIG-003
- **Category:** eligibility
- **Title:** Thin-File and Women-Led SME Inclusivity
- **Tags:** thin file, women-led, affirmative, financial inclusion, bureau, collateral

### Thin-File SME Qualification
SMEs without prior credit bureau records (e-CIB / commercial bureau) or with fewer than 12 months of formal credit history are explicitly welcome under our cash-flow scrutiny model. Eligibility is established through transactional velocity, utility bill punctuality, and verifiable digital receipts rather than requiring physical property collateral.

### Women-Led SME Inclusivity
Enterprises with 50%+ female ownership or day-to-day management qualify for preferential evaluation parameters:
- Dedicated concessionary markup band (up to 200 bps discount on standard rate bands).
- Flexible vintage requirement (minimum 6 months for women-led digital enterprises).
- Targeted technical advisory and streamlined document assistance.
