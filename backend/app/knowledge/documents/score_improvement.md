### What is the Path to Approval?
Rather than a permanent rejection, declined or referred applicants receive a personalized, feasible step-by-step roadmap:
- The algorithm calculates the minimum feasible behavioural adjustments required to cross the **APPROVE threshold (Score 680+ | Tier B)**.
- Each plan specifies concrete operational changes, estimated time horizons (e.g., 60 to 90 days), and the expected point increase.
- Applicants who complete their plan milestones are invited to refresh their score without incurring new application charges.

## Chunk KB-IMPR-002: Actionable Cash-Flow Habits That Raise Your Score
- **Chunk ID:** KB-IMPR-002
- **Category:** how_to_improve
- **Title:** Core Operational Habits to Increase Credit Score
- **Tags:** digital transactions, buffer, deposits, overdraft, cashflow habits

### Top 4 High-Impact Habits
1. **Route Sales Through Digital Channels:** Increasing the ratio of customer receipts via bank transfers, POS terminals, and Raast from 20% to 50%+ significantly lowers unverified cash risk, adding 30 to 60 points.
2. **Maintain a Minimum Closing Balance Buffer:** Avoid draining accounts to near-zero at month-end. Retaining an average cushion equal to 15% to 20% of monthly operating expenses reduces default probability.
3. **Eliminate Cheque Bounces & Overdraft Breaches:** Zero bounced cheques and zero unauthorised overdraft instances over a 90-day window restores trust instantly.
4. **Smooth Working Capital Spikes:** Stagger large supplier payments across the month rather than making concentrated single-day bulk withdrawals.

## Chunk KB-IMPR-003: Bill Payment Punctuality & NTN Formalisation
- **Chunk ID:** KB-IMPR-003
- **Category:** how_to_improve
- **Title:** Utility Punctuality and Tax Record Formalisation
- **Tags:** utility bills, ntn, tax compliance, punctuality, formalisation

### Punctuality as a Proxy for Credit Integrity
- **Timely Utility Payments:** Paying electricity, gas, and commercial broadband bills at least 3 days before the due date creates a positive payment punctuality track record. Even 3 consecutive months of timely utility payments can boost thin-file scores by 25+ points.
- **Active NTN & Tax Records:** Registering an NTN with the FBR and maintaining active taxpayer status (ATL) confirms legal formalisation, which unlocks higher facility limits and lower interest rate bands.
