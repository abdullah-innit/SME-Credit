### Core Mandatory Documents
Applicants must submit digitized copies of the following documents:
1. **National Identity Card (CNIC / SNIC):** Color front and back copies of the primary sponsor and partners/directors.
2. **Bank / Wallet Statements:** 6 to 12 consecutive months of official bank account statements (PDF or CSV e-statements directly downloaded from internet banking) or merchant wallet ledger statements.
3. **National Tax Number (NTN) & Return:** Active NTN registration certificate from FBR. For businesses with turnover exceeding PKR 3,000,000, latest tax return or active taxpayer list (ATL) confirmation is recommended.
4. **Business Premises Evidence:** Rent agreement, utility bill registered under the business name/premises, or property ownership document.
5. **Constitutive Documents:**
   - Sole Proprietorship: Declaration of proprietorship on business letterhead.
   - Partnership: Registered Partnership Deed and Form C.
   - Private Limited Company: Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum & Articles of Association, and Form 29.

## Chunk KB-DOCS-002: Alternative Cash-Flow Verification Records
- **Chunk ID:** KB-DOCS-002
- **Category:** documents
- **Title:** Alternative Cash-Flow Verification and Thin-File Documents
- **Tags:** alternative data, utility bills, digital payments, invoices, pos, merchant

### Alternative Evidentiary Records
For informal or micro-enterprises with incomplete banking history, SME Credit accepts alternative verifiable data:
1. **Utility Invoices:** Paid electricity, gas, commercial water, or internet bills for the preceding 6 months showing payment punctuality.
2. **Digital Point-of-Sale (POS) & Raast Summaries:** Monthly merchant acquiring settlement statements or Raast P2M transaction logs.
3. **Key Supplier Invoices & Receipts:** Consecutive supplier purchase receipts and distributor invoices demonstrating inventory movement.
4. **Third-Party Platform Reports:** For delivery/e-commerce/freelance businesses, digital payout logs from platforms (e.g., Daraz, Foodpanda, Upwork, Careem).
