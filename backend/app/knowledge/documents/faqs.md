### Common Questions & Answers
**Q1: Do I need land or property collateral to get a loan?**
*Answer:* No. SME Credit specializes in cash-flow backed unsecured working capital facilities up to PKR 5,000,000. Your loan capacity is evaluated on real transactional cash flow, customer receipts, and operational discipline rather than fixed asset mortgages.

**Q2: How long does the AI scrutiny and decision process take?**
*Answer:* Automated cash-flow parsing and initial scorecard evaluation take less than 15 seconds once statements are uploaded. For applications receiving an instant APPROVE, final bank officer verification and facility disbursement typically complete within 24 to 48 hours.

**Q3: Can I qualify if I do not have a prior bank credit history (thin file)?**
*Answer:* Yes. Over 35% of eligible SMEs on our platform are thin-file borrowers. We verify business health through your utility bills, digital payment volume, and transaction history.

### What Does "Referred" Mean?
**Q: My application says "Referred". Was my loan rejected?**
*Answer:* No! A "Referred" (Tier C or D) status means the automated engine detected borderline factors (such as sector seasonality, rapid growth spikes, or missing documents) that require human judgment. A senior credit officer reviews your file, may request supplementary invoices or clarification, and often approves the facility with adjusted terms or covenants.

**Q: Can I request a second human review if I disagree with the automated assessment?**
*Answer:* Absolutely. Every applicant has the right to request a formal human underwriter review. You can provide additional financial records, explanations for one-off seasonal dips, or proof of new revenue contracts to support your case.

### Language Support & Data Security
**Q: Can I interact with the platform in Urdu?**
*Answer:* Yes. SME Credit fully supports conversational guidance, credit memo summaries, and adverse-action explanations in Urdu (اردو), Roman Urdu, and English.

**Q: Is my banking data kept confidential and secure?**
*Answer:* Yes. All financial statements are encrypted at rest and in transit. SME Credit uses strict privacy filters: account numbers and CNICs are masked, raw financial identifiers are never sold or shared with third parties, and data is used strictly for credit scrutiny under explicit applicant consent.
