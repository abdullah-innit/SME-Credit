## Chunk KB-TERM-001: Facility Limits and Purpose
- **Chunk ID:** KB-TERM-001
- **Category:** product_terms
- **Title:** Facility Limits and Purpose
- **Tags:** loan limits, facility amount, working capital, inventory, equipment

### Facility Parameters
SME Credit offers short- to medium-term unsecured and semi-secured business financing tailored for liquidity and expansion:
- **Minimum Facility Amount:** PKR 100,000
- **Maximum Facility Amount:** PKR 5,000,000 (up to PKR 10,000,000 for audited corporate SMEs)
- **Approved Limit Formula:** Typically sized at 20% to 35% of verifiable average 90-day cash turnover, ensuring debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) remains comfortably above 1.25x.
- **Eligible Purposes:**
  - Inventory restocking and raw material bulk purchases.
  - Supplier payables settlement to capture early-payment discounts.
  - Seasonal working capital buildup (e.g., Eid collections, harvest cycles).
  - Light machinery / tooling / POS digital infrastructure upgrades.

## Chunk KB-TERM-002: Tenor, Pricing Bands and Amortisation
- **Chunk ID:** KB-TERM-002
- **Category:** product_terms
- **Title:** Tenor, Pricing Bands and Amortisation Structure
- **Tags:** tenor, pricing, interest rate, markup, kibor, amortisation, installments

### Tenor Options
- **Short-Term Liquidity Line:** 3 to 6 months (bullet or quarterly principal amortisation).
- **Core Working Capital Facility:** 12 months (equal monthly instalments of principal + markup).
- **Expansion Facility:** 18 to 24 months (amortised monthly with optional 30-day grace period).

### Indicative Pricing Bands
Pricing is risk-based, calculated transparently based on model risk tier (A to E):
- **Tier A (Lowest Risk, Score 750+):** 1-year KIBOR + 2.50% to 3.50% p.a.
- **Tier B (Prime SME, Score 680-749):** 1-year KIBOR + 3.75% to 4.75% p.a.
- **Tier C (Moderate Risk, Score 620-679):** 1-year KIBOR + 5.00% to 6.50% p.a.
- **Tier D (High Risk, Score 550-619):** Referred for underwriter review; pricing customized with risk mitigants.
- **Tier E (Score < 550):** Outside standard risk appetite; guidance provided on Path to Approval.

## Chunk KB-TERM-003: Fee Structure, No Hidden Charges and Prepayment
- **Chunk ID:** KB-TERM-003
- **Category:** product_terms
- **Title:** Transparent Fee Structure and Early Settlement
- **Tags:** fees, processing fee, prepayment penalty, zero hidden costs, transparency

### Transparent Cost of Credit
- **Application Fee:** PKR 0 (Zero fee to submit application or run credit score).
- **Documentation & Processing Fee:** Flat 0.75% to 1.25% of sanctioned facility amount (capped at PKR 25,000), deducted upon disbursement.
- **Taxes & Stamp Duty:** Levied strictly at actual provincial government rates (no markup or surcharge added by SME Credit).
- **Early Settlement / Prepayment Penalty:** **Zero (0%) prepayment fee**. Borrowers may repay outstanding principal early at any time without penalty, reducing their total markup expense.
