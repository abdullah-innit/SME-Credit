### Responsible Lending Commitment
SME Credit adheres to ethical and responsible credit principles designed to empower small businesses without inducing over-indebtedness:
1. **Capacity-Based Sanctioning:** Facilities are sized strictly on verified net disposable cash flow. We never approve debt that exceeds a sustainable debt service burden (maximum 40% of net operating monthly surplus).
2. **Full Cost Transparency:** Total cost of credit, APR equivalents, processing fees, and payment schedules are presented in plain language prior to contract execution. Zero hidden compounding fees or undisclosed charges.
3. **No Coercive Recovery Practices:** All customer interactions adhere to respectful, ethical standards. Collection practices comply with fair debt treatment and grievance redressal standards.
4. **Borrower Grievance Redressal:** Applicants have the right to request plain-language explanations of decisions, seek human review, and contact consumer protection ombudsmen without prejudice.
5. **Debt Advisory Support:** For stressed businesses, referral to free financial counseling and working capital restructuring advisory is provided.
