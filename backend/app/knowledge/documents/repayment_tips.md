### Managing Facility Cash Flows
1. **Dedicate an Inflow Percentage to Loan Repayment:** Automatically earmark 5% to 10% of daily gross receipts into a designated debt-service reserve account. Setting aside funds daily prevents end-of-month liquidity crunches.
2. **Align Inventory Cycles with Instalment Due Dates:** Ensure supplier credit terms and customer collection windows are aligned so that receivable receipts arrive at least 5 business days prior to your monthly repayment date.
3. **Protect the Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):** Aim to maintain net operating cash flow at least 1.30 times your total monthly loan instalment obligations.

## Chunk KB-REPAY-002: Direct Debit Automation and Seasonal Buffers
- **Chunk ID:** KB-REPAY-002
- **Category:** repayment_tips
- **Title:** Direct Debit Automation and Seasonal Buffer Reserves
- **Tags:** auto debit, direct debit, seasonality, buffer, installment

### Best Practices for Seamless Repayment
- **Set Up Automated Direct Debit / Standing Instruction:** Configure an automatic direct debit mandate with your commercial bank or mobile wallet. This eliminates the risk of inadvertent late payment fees or score markdowns caused by operational oversight.
- **Build Pre-Seasonal Buffers:** For seasonal businesses (e.g., retail or garment shops experiencing post-Eid slumps, or agri-processing with winter troughs), build up a 2-month repayment liquidity reserve during peak revenue months to cushion quiet periods.

## Chunk KB-REPAY-003: Proactive Communication in Liquidity Distress
- **Chunk ID:** KB-REPAY-003
- **Category:** repayment_tips
- **Title:** Proactive Bank Communication During Unexpected Cash Squeezes
- **Tags:** hardship, restructuring, liquidity squeeze, communication, late payment

### What to Do If Facing Unexpected Cash Distress
If your business faces an unexpected cash-flow interruption (e.g., major client payment default, fire, supply disruption):
1. **Contact Your Relationship Officer Early:** Notify your lending officer at least 7 to 10 days before the instalment due date.
2. **Request Temporary Tenor Rescheduling:** SME Credit partner institutions offer temporary grace periods or tenor extensions for transparent borrowers experiencing verifiable temporary hardship.
3. **Never Go Dark:** Uncommunicated late payments trigger negative bureau reporting and penalty markups; proactive dialogue preserves your enterprise credit score.
